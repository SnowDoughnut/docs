One row per competitor. A card is not in circulation until it has a row here.

The library answers a question no single card can: across every deal being worked right now, which competitors are actually in them, which cards are current, and where the same losing reason keeps repeating. Twelve cards written over two years look like coverage until they are listed with their review dates next to them.

<Info>
  Rows are filled from a completed [competitor battle card](/competitor-battle-card). Tier values are defined at the bottom of this page. The reasoning behind cards and tiering is in [battle card concepts](/battle-card-concepts).
</Info>

## Before you write a new card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the register below">
    If the competitor already has a card, update it rather than starting a second one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the deal count">
    A competitor seen in a handful of deals gets a watch row here. A card carries maintenance cost forever, and an unmaintained card costs more credibility than it returns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the card, then add the row">
    A row with no card behind it is a name, not a card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier values

| Tier | Meaning | What it gets |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Tier 1 | Appears in enough deals to move the forecast | A full card, a named owner, quarterly review |
| Tier 2 | Appears regularly, rarely decides the deal | A short card covering sections 1 to 4, reviewed twice a year |
| Watch | Seen occasionally, or new to the category | A register row and a note. No card |
| Retired | No longer seen, acquired, or shut down | The row stays, with the date and what happened |

<Note>
  Retired rows stay in the library. A competitor that disappeared, and the reason, is the entry that stops someone rebuilding a card for a company that no longer sells the product.
</Note>

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| Current | Reviewed within its interval, claims checked |
| Needs review | Past its review date, or a claim on it is in question |
| Draft | Being written, not yet in circulation |
| Retired | Competitor no longer active or no longer seen |

## Review

Cards go stale without announcing it, so the review is scheduled rather than triggered by a loss.

At each review, for every Tier 1 and Tier 2 card:

- Has the competitor shipped anything that makes a claim on the card false? Check their changelog and pricing page, and note the date you read them.
- Do the deal count and win rate still match what the card says about where they win?
- Are the proof points still cleared, and are the named customers still customers?
- Is the same losing reason now appearing against other competitors? See the warning above.
- Is a Tier 2 competitor now appearing often enough to warrant a full card, or a Tier 1 few enough to drop?

<Warning>
  A rep who finds one stale claim on a card stops trusting the whole set, and that costs more than the individual claim ever did. If a card cannot be kept current, retire it and move the competitor to a watch row rather than leaving it in circulation.
</Warning>
