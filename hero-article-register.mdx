Twenty slots. One hero article per authority area, each with a named owner and a scheduled review. An article is not finished until it has a row here.

<Info>
  Areas are chosen using the authority test in the [article library overview](/article-library-overview). Rows are worked through the [article maintenance process](/article-maintenance-process) on a fixed cycle. Default review cadence for a hero article is quarterly.
</Info>

## The register

Fill a row when an area is agreed, before the article is written. Leave the empty slots visible: they are the honest picture of how much of the set is claimed.

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| Area agreed | The slot is claimed, no brief written yet |
| Brief written | Brief filed in the [article brief library](/article-brief-library) |
| Draft | Being written or reviewed |
| Live | Published and maintained |
| Overdue | Past its next review date |
| Demoted | Moved to the [supporting register](/supporting-article-register), slot freed |

## Rules for this register

- **The cap is twenty.** Adding a twenty-first hero article means demoting or retiring one first. A hero set that grows quietly becomes an ordinary content library with an aspirational label.
- **One owner per row, named.** Not a team, not a role.
- **Every live row has a next review date.** A row without one will never appear in a review cycle.
- **A last-updated date means something changed.** Bumping a date without a substantive change removes the signal that tells you which articles need attention.
- **IDs are permanent and never reused.** A demoted article keeps its history in the supporting register notes.
