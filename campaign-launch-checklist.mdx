---
title: "Campaign launch checklist"
description: "The campaign-level pass before anything goes live: brief complete and filed, profile and message pulled from source, naming and UTMs joined, budget and targets set, approvals cleared, and every platform's own pre-launch check passed."
---

Run this once per campaign, after every platform build is complete and paused. It checks the campaign as a whole. The platform builds are checked separately by their own pre-launch checklists.

<Info>
  This sits above the platform checklists, it doesn't replace them. Each platform still runs its own: [Meta](/meta-pre-launch-checklist), [Google](/google-pre-launch-checklist), [LinkedIn](/linkedin-pre-launch-checklist), [TikTok](/tiktok-pre-launch-checklist), [email](/email-pre-send-checklist), [organic social](/organic-social-pre-publish-checklist).
</Info>

## Brief and strategy

- The [marketing campaign brief](/marketing-campaign-brief) is complete, with no section left as an example.
- Every channel the campaign runs on appears in the brief's channels table with a budget line.
- The campaign carries one message pillar from [messaging](/messaging), and every asset carries the same one.
- The customer profile is linked from the [library](/customer-profile-library-overview) by name, not redescribed in the brief.
- Every claim used anywhere in the campaign appears in the approved claims table in [messaging](/messaging).

## Naming and tracking

- Campaign, ad set or ad group, and ad names all follow the [conventions](/naming-conventions-overview).
- Any new field value has been added to the convention page, not just typed into the platform.
- The `utm_campaign` value matches the campaign name value exactly, in the same case.
- Every destination URL is tagged to [UTM standards](/utm-standards), including links in email and organic posts.
- Any new event fired by this campaign exists in the [event tracking plan](/event-tracking-plan) by name.
- A test click on each channel's link resolves to a live page with no redirect chain.

<Warning>
  The naming and UTM join is the one thing that cannot be fixed after launch. Rows collected under a wrong or mismatched name stay wrong forever, and the campaign will appear split across two rows in every report from that day on.
</Warning>

## Creative and copy

- Creative is approved and covers every placement and aspect ratio the campaign runs on.
- Copy sits within the character limits of each channel's template.
- Assets are named to [file naming conventions](/file-naming-conventions) and saved where the [asset directory](/asset-directory) says.
- Logo use complies with [logo usage](/logo-usage), including on any partner or creator asset.
- If the campaign has a destination page, it is live, registered in the [landing page library](/landing-page-library), and its conversion action is in the tracking plan.

## Budget and measurement

- Total budget matches the brief and is split across channels as the brief states.
- Flight dates are set on every platform, including end dates.
- The primary KPI is defined in the [data dictionary](/marketing-reporting-data-dictionary), not invented for this campaign.
- A target value exists for that KPI, so the campaign can be judged against something.
- If the campaign carries a test, the [experiment ID](/experiment-id-convention) is registered and applied to every surface.

## Approvals

- [Brand approval](/brand-approval-process) cleared.
- [Legal review](/legal-review-process) cleared for anything comparative, numeric, priced, or regulated.
- The person who owns the budget knows it is about to start spending.

## Filed before launch

- Campaign brief saved to the [campaigns library](/campaign-library-overview).
- Creative brief saved to the [creative brief library](/creative-brief-library).
- Copy brief saved to the [copy brief library](/copy-brief-library).
- Each platform brief saved alongside them.

## Then launch

- Every platform's own pre-launch checklist has passed.
- Unpause in the order the brief specifies, if the sequence matters.
- Confirm spend and impressions register within the first hour on each platform.
