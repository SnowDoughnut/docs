---
title: "Organic social pre-publish checklist"
description: "The pass before an organic social post goes live: specs met, alt text and captions written, links tagged and working, approvals recorded, calendar row ready."
---

Run this before scheduling or publishing. It is short because organic posts are made often, and it covers the things that are awkward to fix once a post is live.

<Info>
  This checklist assumes the [organic social post process](/organic-social-post-process) has been followed and the [organic social post brief](/organic-social-post-brief) is complete.
</Info>

## Content

- The caption matches the [brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone) workbook.
- No placeholder text remains.
- Any product claim is one the company can support, and has been reviewed if it needed review.
- Any customer name, quote, or logo has permission on record.
- The hook works as the first line or first frame, not after a scroll or a tap.

## Creative

- Built to the platform [creative specs](/image-and-video-specs) at the right ratio, not cropped from another format.
- **Alt text written** for every image and every carousel card.
- **Captions on video**, burned in or uploaded, since most viewers watch with sound off.
- Logos and text sit clear of the platform interface on full-screen formats.
- Previewed on a real device, not only on desktop.

## Links and tracking

- Every link works and points at a live page.
- Links carry [UTMs](/utm-standards) with an organic medium value.
- The link is in the place the brief specified, caption or first comment, consistently across the series.
- No link points at a staging or preview URL.

## Approval

- Approvals recorded, per the [approval process](/approval-process), including any [brand](/brand-approval-process) or [legal](/legal-review-process) review the post needed.
- The owner has confirmed the post against the brief.

## Publishing

- Correct account selected. Check this on any shared or agency scheduler.
- Scheduled date, time, and timezone are correct.
- The post is scheduled to the channels the brief names, and no others.
- Tagged accounts and mentions are correct and spelled right.

## After it goes live

- Published URL pasted into the brief.
- Row added to the content calendar with channel, format, date, owner, and URL.
- A review point set to check whether the post is worth running as paid creative.
