title: "Make a campaign"
description: "The full path from campaign idea to live campaign: which documents to fill, what to name things, where to save each output, and what to link it from. Hands off to the platform process for the build itself."
---

Use this when you're planning a campaign that will run in a paid platform. It's the channel-agnostic path. The build step hands off to the process for whichever platform you're running on.

<Note>
  Setup stages 1 to 3 in [get started](/overview) have to be done first. Without naming conventions and a brand system, you'll be inventing both while under deadline.
</Note>

## Before you start

| You need | Where it lives |
| :-- | :-- |
| A positioning statement and message pillars | [Positioning](/positioning), [messaging](/messaging) |
| A named customer profile | [Customer profile library](/customer-profile-library-overview) |
| Naming conventions decided | [Naming conventions overview](/naming-conventions-overview) |
| UTM standard decided | [UTM standards](/utm-standards) |
| A budget | [Budget planning](/budget-planning) |

## The path

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Fill in the campaign brief">
    Copy the [marketing campaign brief](/marketing-campaign-brief) and fill it in. Pull the customer profile from the library by name, and pull the message pillar from [messaging](/messaging). Don't rewrite either.

    Full detail: [campaign brief process](/campaign-brief-process).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. Name the campaign">
    Build the campaign name from [campaign naming conventions](/campaign-naming-conventions) before you build anything in a platform. Renaming later breaks the join between platform data and your reporting.

    Then name the ad sets or ad groups from [ad set, ad group and variant naming conventions](/ad-set-group-naming-conventions), and the ads from [ad name conventions](/ad-name-conventions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. Build the tracking">
    Tag every destination link using [UTM standards](/utm-standards). The UTM values have to match the campaign name values, or platform spend and analytics conversions cannot be joined.

    If the campaign fires new events, add them to the [event tracking plan](/event-tracking-plan) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="4. Check what already exists">
    Before briefing anything new, check the [asset directory](/asset-directory). An approved asset that already exists, or crops to fit, is faster than a brief and has cleared review. Same for words: search the [copy drive](/copy-drive) before writing fresh.
  </Step>
  <Step title="5. Brief the creative and copy">
    Creative concept goes in a [creative brief](/creative-brief), following the [creative brief process](/creative-brief-process). The words go in a [copy brief](/ad-copy-standards). Both link back to the campaign brief rather than restating the strategy.

    Walkthroughs: [brief creative](/brief-creative) and [write copy](/write-copy).

    If the campaign needs a destination page, check the [landing page library](/landing-page-library) first, then see [build a landing page](/build-a-landing-page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="6. Get approvals">
    Brand sign-off runs through the [brand approval process](/brand-approval-process). Anything with a claim, a price, or a comparison also goes through [legal review](/legal-review-process).
  </Step>
  <Step title="7. Build it in the platform">
    Hand off to the process for your platform. Each one covers the platform brief, the build levels, the specs, and the pre-launch checklist.

    | Platform | Process | Brief | Final check |
    | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
    | Meta | [Launching a Meta campaign](/meta-campaign-process) | [Meta campaign brief](/meta-campaign-brief) | [Pre-launch checklist](/meta-pre-launch-checklist) |
    | Google | [Launching a Google campaign](/google-campaign-process) | [Google campaign brief](/google-campaign-brief) | [Pre-launch checklist](/google-pre-launch-checklist) |
    | LinkedIn | [Launching a LinkedIn campaign](/linkedin-campaign-process) | [LinkedIn campaign brief](/linkedin-campaign-brief) | [Pre-launch checklist](/linkedin-pre-launch-checklist) |
    | TikTok | [Launching a TikTok campaign](/tiktok-campaign-process) | [TikTok campaign brief](/tiktok-campaign-brief) | [Pre-launch checklist](/tiktok-pre-launch-checklist) |
    | Email | [Launching an email campaign](/email-campaign-process) | [Email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) | [Pre-send checklist](/email-pre-send-checklist) |
    | Organic social | [Publishing a post](/organic-social-post-process) | [Post brief](/organic-social-post-brief) | [Pre-publish checklist](/organic-social-pre-publish-checklist) |
  </Step>
  <Step title="8. QA and launch">
    Run the [campaign QA checklist](/campaign-qa-checklist) and the [campaign launch checklist](/campaign-launch-checklist) across the whole campaign, alongside the platform's own pre-launch pass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="9. File everything">
    Save the campaign brief to the [campaigns library](/campaign-library-overview). Save the creative brief to the [creative brief library](/creative-briefs) and the copy brief to the [copy brief library](/copy-brief-library). A brief nobody filed is a campaign nobody can learn from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="10. Report and close out">
    The campaign's rows flow into the [flat file](/flat-file-template) and appear in [weekly reports](/weekly-reporting-process) automatically if the naming and UTMs are right.

    When the campaign ends, run the [campaign retrospective](/campaign-retrospect-process) and link the outcome back to the brief in the library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should have at the end

- A campaign brief in the campaigns library, with the outcome linked to it.
- A platform brief filed alongside it.
- Platform names that match your conventions exactly, unedited since build.
- UTMs that join cleanly to those names in the flat file.
- Creative and copy briefs in their libraries, with the finished assets linked from them.
