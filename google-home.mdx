Google covers Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen, Display, YouTube, and app campaigns. Only the platform-specific detail lives here. Strategy, naming, tracking, and brand rules are shared.

## Launching a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow the process">
    [**Launching a Google campaign**](/google-campaign-process) covers every campaign type, from Search to Performance Max, and lists every input that has to exist first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the brief">
    The [**Google campaign brief**](/google-campaign-brief) fixes campaign type, conversion goal, budget, bid strategy, and structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check before you spend">
    The [**Google pre-launch checklist**](/google-pre-launch-checklist) is the last pass with the campaign built and paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>
