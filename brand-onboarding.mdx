Read this before producing anything the brand's name goes on. It takes about an hour and saves the review round where someone tells you the tone is wrong.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. What the brand claims">
    [Positioning](/positioning) is the strategic claim: who it's for, what it competes against, what makes it different. [Messaging](/messaging) turns that into approved words, pillars, and a claims table.

    Almost every "can we say this?" question is answered by the claims table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. How the brand sounds">
    [Brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone). Section 4 is the one to bookmark: it pairs off-brand and on-brand versions of real lines, which is faster to check against than a list of traits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. How the brand looks">
    [Visual identity](/visual-identity) for color, type, spacing, imagery and icons. [Logo usage](/logo-usage) for anything involving the logo, including the misuse list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="4. Where the files are">
    [Brand assets](/brand-assets) holds the register: what exists, where it lives, who owns it, and what you're allowed to use it for. Never use a file from a deck, a screenshot, or a search result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="5. How work gets briefed and approved">
    [Brief creative](/brief-creative) for visuals, [write copy](/write-copy) for words. Sign-off runs through the [brand approval process](/brand-approval-process), and anything with a claim or a price also goes through [legal review](/legal-review-process).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which document answers which question

| Question | Document |
| :-- | :-- |
| Can we say this? | The claims table in [messaging](/messaging) |
| Does this sound right? | Section 4 of [brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone) |
| What color or font is this? | [Visual identity](/visual-identity) |
| Can I put the logo there? | [Logo usage](/logo-usage) |
| Where's the current file? | [Brand assets](/brand-assets) |
| Why is it like this? | [Brand strategy concepts](/brand-strategy-concepts), [visual identity concepts](/visual-identity-concepts) |
| Not covered anywhere? | [Brand guidelines](/brand-guidelines) |
