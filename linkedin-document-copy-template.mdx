<Note>
  A hook written fresh here belongs back in the angle's brief once it is approved, so the next writer starts from it rather than reinventing it.
</Note>

An ungated document reads and downloads in feed with no form, and suits awareness and engagement objectives. A gated document shows the preview pages, then asks for a lead gen form to unlock the rest; it needs the lead generation objective and a document of at least two pages. Checked 30 October 2026.
