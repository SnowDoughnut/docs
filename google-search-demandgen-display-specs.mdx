The Google formats not covered by [Performance Max](/google-pmax-creative-specs) or [responsive display](/google-display-creative-specs). Current as of 28 July 2026.

## Responsive search ads

No image or video creative is required. Image assets are added separately.

| Field | Limit | Quantity |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Headlines | 30 characters | 1–15 |
| Descriptions | 90 characters | 1–4 |
| Display paths | 15 characters each | 2, optional |

## Demand Gen

| Asset | Spec |
| :-- | :-- |
| Images | 1200 × 628 · 1200 × 1200 · 960 × 1200 |
| Videos | 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, or 4:5 |
| Video length | 10–60 seconds recommended |

| Field | Limit | Quantity |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Headlines | 40 characters | 1–5 |
| Descriptions | 90 characters | 1–5 |
| Business name | 25 characters | 1 |

<Warning>
  Demand Gen headlines allow 40 characters, unlike the 30 elsewhere in Google Ads. Include at least one headline of **30 characters or fewer** so the ad stays eligible to serve on Display.
</Warning>

## Uploaded display ads

GIF, JPG, or PNG. **Maximum 150 KB.** Copy is baked into the creative. Animated GIFs must finish within 30 seconds and run below 5 FPS.

## Working notes

- **150 KB is the real constraint.** At 970 × 250 that is tight; flatten gradients and limit animation frames rather than dropping quality across the board.
- **Start from the four highest-traffic sizes** — 300 × 250, 728 × 90, 160 × 600, 320 × 50 — then extend the set once the layout survives all four.
- **Type has to work at 50 px tall.** Design the mobile banner first; if the message fits there it will scale up.
