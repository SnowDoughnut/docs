A template is a blank sheet you copy and fill in. Everything on this page is one of those. The concepts and standards pages at the bottom hold the reasoning behind what goes in them.

## Article templates

| Template | Use it for |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Article writing template](/article-writing-template) | Drafting one article, with its publishing metadata alongside the draft. |

The template holds the draft. What the draft has to obey (heading hierarchy, length, structure ratios, slugs, metadata, links, schema, and dates) lives in [article standards](/article-standards), so the numbers stay in one place.

## Registers

A register is a table you add a row to. Templates and registers both get filled in as part of publishing an article.

| Register | What it holds |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Hero article register](/hero-article-register) | The capped set of 20 hero articles. |
| [Supporting article register](/supporting-article-register) | Everything else. |
| [Article brief library](/article-brief-library) | Where completed briefs are filed. |
| [Content calendar](/content-calendar) | When each article is planned for. |

## Templates that apply on every platform

| Template | Use it for |
| :-- | :-- |
| [Landing page skeleton](/landing-page-skeleton) | A page that has to convert. See the split below. |
| [Copy templates](/copy-templates) | The index of every platform's copy templates. |
| [Flat file template](/flat-file-template) | The one spreadsheet every report reads from. |
| [Weekly](/weekly-report-template), [monthly](/monthly-report-template), [quarterly](/quarterly-report-template), [annual](/annual-report-template) report templates | Reporting on what the article library did. |

<Note>
  An article and a landing page are different things and use different templates. An article exists to be found by someone searching; a landing page exists to produce one action from traffic you paid to send. If the page has to rank, it's an article.
</Note>

## Not templates

These get filled in too. They hold the decisions and the checks, and the templates above hold the finished artifact:

- [GEO checklist](/geo-checklist) The citability pass.
- [Article update checklist](/article-update-checklist) The pass for an article being refreshed.
