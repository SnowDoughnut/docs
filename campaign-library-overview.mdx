This library holds completed [marketing campaign briefs](/marketing-campaign-brief) and the platform campaign briefs underneath them, filed using [campaign naming conventions](/campaign-naming-conventions) once a campaign is planned and running. Use it to find how a past campaign was structured, what it targeted, and how it performed.

Briefs are filed at launch rather than at close, so the next person building a campaign can see how the last one was structured while it is still running.

## How it's organized

The marketing campaign brief sits at the top. Each platform campaign brief it generates is filed in a folder for that platform.

```text
campaigns/
└── 2026-fresh-batch-trial/
    ├── marketing-campaign-brief
    ├── meta/
    ├── google/
    ├── tiktok/
    ├── linkedin/
    ├── email/
    └── organic-social/
```

One marketing campaign usually generates several platform briefs. Keeping them in platform folders under their parent means you can read a campaign two ways: everything one campaign did, or everything one platform has been used for.

## What gets filed here

| Document | Filed as | Produced by |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Marketing campaign brief](/marketing-campaign-brief) | One per campaign, at the top level | [Campaign brief process](/campaign-brief-process) |
| [Meta campaign brief](/meta-campaign-brief) | In the Meta folder | [Launching a Meta campaign](/meta-campaign-process) |
| [Google campaign brief](/google-campaign-brief) | In the Google folder | [Launching a Google campaign](/google-campaign-process) |
| [TikTok campaign brief](/tiktok-campaign-brief) | In the TikTok folder | [Launching a TikTok campaign](/tiktok-campaign-process) |
| [LinkedIn campaign brief](/linkedin-campaign-brief) | In the LinkedIn folder | [Launching a LinkedIn campaign](/linkedin-campaign-process) |
| [Email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) | In the Email folder | [Launching an email campaign](/email-campaign-process) |
| [Organic social post brief](/organic-social-post-brief) | In the Organic social folder | [Publishing an organic social post](/organic-social-post-process) |

Cancelled campaigns are filed too, with a short note on why they stopped. The targeting, creative, and structure decisions in them usually get reused.

## What doesn't get filed here

| Document | Goes in |
| :-- | :-- |
| Creative briefs | [Creative brief library](/creative-briefs) |
| Copy briefs | [Copy brief library](/copy-brief-library) |
| Article briefs | [Article brief library](/article-brief-library) |
| Experiment reviews | [Experiment learning library](/experirment-learning-home) |
| Finished creative and copy | The [creative](/creative-drive), [video](/video-drive), and [copy](/copy-drive) drives |
