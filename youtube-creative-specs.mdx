The dashed red line at 5 seconds is the one that shapes the edit: on skippable in-stream, **everything that must land has to land before it.**

| Format | Duration | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Bumper** | Max 6 seconds | Cannot be skipped. One message, one visual idea |
| **Skippable in-stream** | Minimum 7 seconds | Viewer can skip after 5 s. 15 s is a strong reusable target |
| **Non-skippable in-stream** | 7–15 seconds | Video Reach campaigns can also use 6, 15, and 30 s assets |
| **Non-skippable (connected TV)** | 16–30 seconds | **Horizontal only** |
| **In-feed video** | Any length | Ideal length depends on objective |
| **Shorts** | Minimum 5 seconds | 6–60 s recommended for Video Reach Shorts inventory |

## Video assets

| Orientation | Build at |
| :-- | :-- |
| Horizontal 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 |
| Vertical 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Square 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 |

Minimum recommended quality **720p**. Maximum file size 256 GB.

## Images

| Asset | Spec |
| :-- | :-- |
| In-feed thumbnail | 1280 × 720 (16:9), JPG/GIF/PNG, under 2 MB |
| Companion banner | 300 × 60, JPG/GIF/PNG, max 150 KB. **Desktop only** |

The companion banner can be uploaded or generated from your YouTube channel banner.

## Copy limits

| Placement | Field | Limit |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| In-feed video | Headline | Up to 2 lines of 40 characters |
| In-feed video | Description | Up to 2 lines of 35 characters |
| Shorts | Headline | 40 characters |
| Shorts | Description | 90 characters |

In-feed text may not display on every device or placement, so do not put anything load-bearing there.

## Working notes

- **Front-load the first five seconds.** Brand, product, and the main message go before the skip button appears.
- **A 15-second horizontal master is the most reusable single asset.** It clears the skippable minimum and the non-skippable range.
- **Connected TV is horizontal only.** A vertical-only campaign forfeits that inventory.
