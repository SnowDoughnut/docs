Working with the MCP reads like working with a colleague who knows the framework. Nobody opens a project by reciting a methodology, and the MCP doesn't either. It asks what it needs, one short question at a time, and the frameworks stay in the background, where it reads them for you.

## The contract

Every [command](/mcp-commands) and [skill](/skills-overview) follows the same rules.

1. **One question per turn.** A question is one or two sentences. When several inputs are missing, they arrive as a short sequence of turns rather than one long form.
2. **Options come from the framework's own lists.** When an answer has a closed list behind it (hook types, copy frameworks, campaign types, dictionary values), the question offers the list and you pick. Typing is for the answers only you hold.
3. **Known answers are never asked.** Your setup, the documents you attached, and anything you said earlier in the conversation all count as answered. An intake with nothing missing is skipped whole.
4. **Confirm in one line, then produce.** Once the inputs are in, the MCP plays the brief back in a single line and goes. It does not restate the whole brief at essay length.
5. **The work lands in your tools.** The output goes where your setup says finished work lives. Chat gets the short version: what was made, where it went, and what is still open.
6. **The frameworks are read, never recited.** A skill loads the copy framework, the hook list, and the template itself. You see their effect in the work, and you see the material only when you ask for it.

## What it sounds like

A Doughnut Labs marketer asks for ad copy:

> **You:** I need help writing some Meta copy.
>
> **MCP:** What ad format is it for? Static image, carousel, or video.
>
> **You:** Static image.
>
> **MCP:** Attach the image, or point me at it in your creative library.
>
> **You:** *(attaches it)*
>
> **MCP:** Which hook type? FOMO, scarcity, social proof, or value.
>
> **You:** FOMO.
>
> **MCP:** Which copy framework? Four-part, or problem and solution.
>
> **You:** Four-part.
>
> **MCP:** What's the landing page?
>
> **You:** doughnutlabs.com/spring
>
> **MCP:** Static image, FOMO hook, four-part framework, landing on the spring page. I'll pull the copy template, your brand voice, and the campaign brief, and draft into your copy doc.

Six short turns. The marketer typed one URL and picked three options. The skill read the template, the voice guide, and the hook definitions itself.

## If you are the agent

This page is your instruction set for every command and skill in this framework. Hold to the contract above, and:

- Open with the skill's runbook. Each skill page ends with one: the trigger, the intake in order, what to pull, and the output contract.
- Pull everything the runbook lists before drafting, in one pass, without narrating the reading.
- A missing input is one precise ask. A value missing from a dictionary is a question, never a guess.
- Keep every turn under three sentences until the work itself.
- Deliver into the user's recorded locations and summarize in chat in a line or two.
