Every figure on this page comes from Google's own specification pages, checked 28 July 2026. Sources are listed at the bottom.

<Note>
  Responsive display ads are asset-based. You supply headlines, descriptions, images, and logos; Google assembles them into layouts across the Display Network. You are designing components, not a finished banner.
</Note>

## Image assets

| Ratio | Recommended | Minimum | Quantity | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Horizontal **1.91:1** | 1200 × 628 | 600 × 314 | 1–15 (5 recommended) | Yes |
| Square **1:1** | 600 × 600 | 300 × 300 | 1–15 (5 recommended) | Yes |
| Logo **1:1** | 1200 × 1200 | 128 × 128 | 1–5 (1 recommended) | No |
| Logo **4:1** | 1200 × 300 | 512 × 128 | 1–5 (1 recommended) | No |

<Warning>
  The square recommendation here is **600 × 600**, not the 1200 × 1200 that Performance Max recommends. If you reuse one asset library across both, export the square at 1200 × 1200 — it satisfies both, since it clears the display minimum comfortably.
</Warning>

## Video assets

| Ratio | Orientation | Length | Quantity | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **16:9** | Horizontal | Any, 30 seconds preferred | 1–5 (2 recommended) | No |
| **1:1** | Square | Any, 30 seconds preferred | 1–5 (2 recommended) | No |

## Text assets

| Type | Limit | Quantity | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Headlines | 30 characters | 1–5 | Yes |
| Long headline | 90 characters | 1 | Yes |
| Descriptions | 90 characters | 1–5 | Yes |
| Business name | 25 characters | 1 | Yes |
| Call to action | Automated | 1 | Yes |

## How display differs from Performance Max

|  | Responsive display | Performance Max |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Square recommended | 600 × 600 | 1200 × 1200 |
| Vertical 4:5 | Not used | 960 × 1200 |
| Headlines | 1–5 | Up to 15 (11\+ recommended) |
| Video length | 30 seconds preferred | 10 seconds minimum |
| Video orientations | 16:9, 1:1 | 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 |

## Working notes

- **Design for unknown crops.** Your assets are recombined into many layouts and sizes. Anything near an edge is at risk; centre the logo and focal subject.
- **Avoid baked-in text.** Google adds its own headline and description. Text burned into the image competes with them and often reads as clutter at small sizes.
- **Supply both required ratios properly.** Uploading only 1.91:1 leaves Google to crop a square itself, usually badly.
