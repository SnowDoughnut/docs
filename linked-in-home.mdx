LinkedIn is the main paid channel for B2B. Only the platform-specific detail lives here. Strategy, naming, tracking, and brand rules are shared.

## Launching a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow the process">
    [**Launching a LinkedIn campaign**](/linkedin-campaign-process) covers campaign group, campaign, and ad, and lists every input that has to exist first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the brief">
    The [**LinkedIn campaign brief**](/linkedin-campaign-brief) fixes objective, conversion action, budget, bid strategy, audience, and format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check before you spend">
    The [**LinkedIn pre-launch checklist**](/linkedin-pre-launch-checklist) is the last pass with the campaign built and paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>
