---
title: "Creative review checklist"
description: "How to review a delivered asset against the brief that commissioned it: does it carry the message, does it meet the brand rules, does it meet the specs, and is it cleared to ship."
---

Use this when a piece of creative comes back and someone has to approve it. Review against what the brief asked for. Taste is not a review criterion, and "I'd have done it differently" is not a revision request.

<Info>
  This is step 7 of the [creative brief process](/creative-brief-process). It assumes a filled [creative brief](/creative-brief) exists. If there's no brief, there's nothing to review against, and that's the first thing to fix.
</Info>

## Against the brief

- The asset does the job the brief's objective names.
- It carries the one message the brief specified, not a nearby one.
- The proof point the brief required is visible or audible, not implied.
- It speaks to the customer profile the brief named, from the [library](/customer-profile-library-overview).
- Every mandatory element in the brief is present.
- Nothing on the brief's "must not appear" list is present.

## Brand

- Colors are from the palette in [visual identity](/visual-identity), at the values recorded there.
- Type is set in the brand typefaces, at a size on the [type scale](/visual-identity).
- Text over image meets the contrast pairings in [visual identity](/visual-identity). Check the worst spot, not the average.
- The logo is a correct variant for its background, per [logo usage](/logo-usage).
- Clear space around the logo is respected, and the logo is above minimum size at the smallest placement it will appear in.
- Nothing on the [misuse list](/logo-usage) has been done to the logo, including a retyped wordmark.
- Imagery matches the direction in [visual identity](/visual-identity), including what that section says to avoid.

## Copy on the asset

- Voice matches the relevant section of [brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone).
- Every claim appears in the approved claims table in [messaging](/messaging).
- Any number, comparison, or superlative has cleared [legal review](/legal-review-process).
- The call to action states the action, not a mood.

## Specs and formats

- Every deliverable the brief listed has been supplied, not just the hero format.
- Dimensions, aspect ratios, and file sizes meet the platform spec: [Meta](/meta-creative-specs), [Google](/google-search-demandgen-display-specs), [LinkedIn](/linkedin-creative-specs), [TikTok](/tiktok-creative-specs), [email](/email-creative-specs), or the shared [image and video specs](/image-and-video-specs).
- Safe zones are respected. Check the crop that will actually run, not the master.
- Video has captions, and works with sound off.
- Images have alt text written.

<Warning>
  Check the smallest and most cropped placement before approving. An asset that reads well at full size and loses its logo or its text in a square crop is not approved creative, it's a master file that still needs work.
</Warning>

## Before approval

- Source files are named to [file naming conventions](/file-naming-conventions).
- Files are saved where the [asset directory](/asset-directory) says they go, not attached to a message.
- Rights and licensing are recorded in [brand assets](/brand-assets), including any expiry on stock, music, or a customer's likeness.
- The finished assets are linked from the brief in the [creative brief library](/creative-briefs).

## Giving feedback

- Each revision request cites the brief line it comes from.
- Requests are batched into one round, not sent as they occur to people.
- Anything that isn't in the brief but genuinely matters gets added to the brief first, so the change is visible rather than remembered.
