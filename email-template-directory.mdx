A template is a blank sheet you copy and fill in. Everything on this page is one of those. The briefs and process pages at the bottom hold the reasoning behind what goes in them.

## Copy templates

One filled template is one send. It holds the field structure and character guidance; the words come from the [copy brief](/copy-brief).

| Format | Template |
| :-- | :-- |
| Promotional and lifecycle sends | [Email copy template](/email-copy-template) |

One template covers both a broadcast and a step in an automated sequence, because the fields are the same either way. What changes is the trigger, which the [email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) records.

## Not templates

These get filled in too. They hold the decisions and the checks, and the templates above hold the finished artifact:

- [Email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) Send type, conversion action, segment, content, send settings, and deliverability.
- [Email pre-send checklist](/email-pre-send-checklist) The last pass. Email is the one channel with no pause button.
- [Email campaign process](/email-campaign-process) The order the work happens in.
- [Email creative specs](/email-creative-specs) Image dimensions and file requirements for a send.
