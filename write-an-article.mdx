## The path

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Read the concepts once">
    [SEO and GEO concepts](/seo-and-geo-concepts) covers why articles are structured the way they are, for both search results and model-generated answers. [Article brief concepts](/article-brief-concepts) covers why articles are briefed separately from campaign copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. Confirm the article belongs">
    Check it maps to an authority area and isn't already covered. See [article library overview](/article-library-overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. Fill in the article brief">
    Work through the [article brief](/article-brief): the question it answers, what new information it carries, who it's for, and the section structure.

    Full detail: [article process](/article-process).
  </Step>
  <Step title="4. Gather the proof">
    An article that restates what already ranks has no reason to exist. Record where each claim comes from as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="5. File the brief and draft">
    Save the brief to the [article brief library](/article-brief-library), then draft in the [article writing template](/article-writing-template). Structure follows [article standards](/article-standards): each section answers its own heading, so it survives being pulled out on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="6. Brief any creative">
    Diagrams, screenshots and images go through a [creative brief](/creative-brief). See [brief creative](/brief-creative).
  </Step>
  <Step title="7. Check before publishing">
    Run the [article pre-publish checklist](/article-pre-publish-checklist), then the [GEO checklist](/geo-checklist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="8. Publish and register">
    Record it in the [hero article register](/hero-article-register) or [supporting article register](/supporting-article-register), with its review date. Link it from related articles and from the library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="9. Set the review date">
    Articles decay without announcing it. The [article maintenance process](/article-maintenance-process) covers the review cycle, and the [article update checklist](/article-update-checklist) covers what to check when the date comes up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you should have at the end

- A brief in the article brief library.
- A published article registered as hero or supporting, with a review date.
- Internal links both to and from related articles.
