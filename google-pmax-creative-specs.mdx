<Note>

</Note>

## Build once, export three times

PMax requires three image ratios. All three crop cleanly out of a single 1200 × 1200 master, landing on Google's recommended size in each case.

## Image assets

| Ratio | Recommended | Minimum | Max file | Upload | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Horizontal **1.91:1** | 1200 × 628 | 600 × 314 | 5120 KB | 4\+ (up to 20) | Yes |
| Square **1:1** | 1200 × 1200 | 300 × 300 | 5120 KB | 4\+ (up to 20) | Yes |
| Vertical **4:5** | 960 × 1200 | 480 × 600 | 5120 KB | 2\+ (up to 20) | No |
| Square logo **1:1** | 1200 × 1200 | 128 × 128 | 5120 KB | 1 (up to 5) | Yes |
| Horizontal logo **4:1** | 1200 × 300 | 512 × 128 | 5120 KB | 1 (up to 5) | No |

Format: `.jpg` or `.png`, 5 MB maximum.

<Note>
  Google publishes no percentage safe zones for these formats, unlike Meta. Center the focal subject and assume any edge can be cropped.
</Note>

### Logo tips

- Upload logos as **square**. A transparent background is often rendered on white, so a predominantly white logo on transparency will disappear.
- If a logo is flagged for blurriness, re-upload a higher-quality file. If issues persist, remove the other ratios and keep only the one that meets spec.

## Video assets

| Ratio | Orientation | Length | Upload | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **16:9** | Horizontal | 10 seconds minimum | 1 of each orientation (max 15) | No |
| **1:1** | Square | 10 seconds minimum | 1 of each orientation (max 15) | No |
| **9:16** | Vertical | 10 seconds minimum | 1 of each orientation (max 15) | No |

A 1920 × 1920 square master holds all three orientations, with a 1080 × 1080 center common to every crop. Frame the action there and one edit works in all three.

<Warning>
  Video is marked optional, but leaving it out has a cost: Google auto-generates video from your other assets. To reach **Excellent** Ad Strength and stop auto-generation, upload your own video in **all three** orientations.
</Warning>

## Text assets

| Type | Limit | Recommended | Required |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Headlines | 30 characters | 11\+ (min 3, max 15) | Yes |
| Long headline | 90 characters | 2\+ (min 1, max 5) | Yes |
| Descriptions | 90 characters | 4\+ (min 2, max 5) | Yes |
| Business name | 25 characters | 1 | Yes |
| Call to action | Automated, or pick from a list | 1 | Yes |
| Final URL | n/a | 1 | Yes |
| Display URL path | 15 characters each | 2 (min 1, max 2) | No |

- Include at least one headline of **15 characters or fewer**.
- Aim for long headlines of **at least 30 characters**.
- Business name must match your domain or verified business name, with no promotional text.
- In Korean, Japanese, and Chinese each character counts as **2** toward the limit.

## Working notes

- **Feed the variety.** More distinct assets give Google more combinations to test. Duplicating one image across ratios wastes that.
- **One clean image per ratio.** Google recommends at least one image without text overlay in each ratio, since overlays clash with placements that add their own chrome.
- **Watch the square logo minimum.** At 128 × 128 it is far below the 1200 × 1200 recommendation. Meeting only the minimum will look soft on large placements.
