A template is a blank sheet you copy and fill in. Everything on this page is one of those. The briefs and concepts pages at the bottom hold the reasoning behind what goes in them.

## Copy templates

One filled template is one ad. Each holds the field structure and character limits for a single format; the words come from the [copy brief](/copy-brief).

| Format | Template |
| :-- | :-- |
| Static and video | [Meta Static/Video ad copy template](/meta-static-video-copy-template) |
| Carousel | [Meta Carousel copy template](/meta-carousel-copy-template) |
| Collection | [Meta Collection copy template](/meta-collection-copy-template) |
| Lead ad | [Meta Lead ad copy template](/meta-lead-ad-copy-template) |

## Not templates

These get filled in too. They hold the decisions and the checks, and the templates above hold the finished artifact:

- [Meta campaign brief](/meta-campaign-brief) The campaign's decisions, fixed before anything is built.
- [Meta pre-launch checklist](/meta-pre-launch-checklist) The last pass with the campaign built and paused.
- [Meta campaign process](/meta-campaign-process) The order the work happens in.
- [Meta creative specs](/meta-creative-specs) Dimensions, safe zones, and file requirements by placement.
