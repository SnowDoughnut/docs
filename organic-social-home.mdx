## What's different about organic

| | Paid | Organic |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Volume of review | Every ad is briefed and approved | Most posts are not |
| Targeting | A named customer profile | Whoever follows you |
| Failure mode | Wasted spend | Voice drift |
| Measurement | Attributed to a campaign | Only measurable if links are tagged |

Voice drift is the real risk. Nobody sets out to sound different; it happens one unreviewed post at a time. The tone-by-channel matrix in [brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone) is where a team decides how much personality organic gets, so the answer exists before someone has to improvise it.

## Tagging organic links

Any link in a bio, a post, or a story needs tagging from [UTM standards](/utm-standards), or the traffic lands in reporting as direct and the channel looks like it does nothing.
