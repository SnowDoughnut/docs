A template is a blank sheet you copy and fill in. Everything on this page is one of those. The briefs and concepts pages at the bottom hold the reasoning behind what goes in them.

## Copy templates

One filled template is one ad. Each holds the field structure and character limits for a single format; the words come from the [copy brief](/copy-brief).

| Format | Template |
| :-- | :-- |
| In-Feed | [TikTok In-Feed ad copy template](/tiktok-in-feed-copy-template) |
| Spark Ad | [TikTok Spark ad copy template](/tiktok-spark-ad-copy-template) |
| Carousel | [TikTok Carousel ad copy template](/tiktok-carousel-copy-template) |
| Video Shopping | [TikTok Video Shopping ad copy template](/tiktok-video-shopping-copy-template) |
| TopView | [TikTok TopView ad copy template](/tiktok-topview-copy-template) |

<Note>
  A Spark ad runs through a creator's own account, so the brand rules that govern an in-feed ad you produced apply differently. Check the third-party section of [logo usage](/logo-usage) before filling that template.
</Note>

## Not templates

These get filled in too. They hold the decisions and the checks, and the templates above hold the finished artifact:

- [TikTok campaign brief](/tiktok-campaign-brief) The campaign's decisions, fixed before anything is built.
- [TikTok pre-launch checklist](/tiktok-pre-launch-checklist) The last pass with the campaign built and paused.
- [TikTok campaign process](/tiktok-campaign-process) The order the work happens in.
- [TikTok creative specs](/tiktok-creative-specs) Dimensions, safe zones, video length, and file requirements.
