Turns source footage, or one finished master video, into an edit brief and a cut list: which cuts to make, at which ratios and lengths, opening with which hook, captioned how. The edit itself runs in your video tool, by the [creator](/glossary#creator) your defaults name.

## When it runs

Ask for video cuts in any form and this skill loads: "cut the launch video for Reels", "we need a 15-second version", "make the vertical edits", or footage handed over with "turn this into ads".

It plans and briefs the edit. Rendering, color, and sound stay in your editing tool.

## The three rules it enforces

<Steps>
  <Step title="The hook is the first seconds, readable with sound off">
    Feed and vertical placements autoplay silent mid-scroll. Every cut opens on its [hook](/hook-types) inside the first seconds, carries captions, and keeps text clear of the platform interface areas the spec pages mark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One real edit per ratio">
    A vertical placement gets a dedicated vertical edit exported at full resolution, never an upscaled crop of the feed master. A 16:9 placement gets a real landscape edit, never a pillarboxed vertical. The ratios come from the platform's specs page, and safe zones come from the platform's current template, since they move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cuts are variants by opening">
    Where the plan calls for variants, the body of the edit is shared and the opening changes: a different hook on the same angle per cut. That is what makes the results readable, and a variant added later to see which performs better needs a registered experiment ID first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it asks you for

One question per turn, following [how the MCP talks](/mcp-conversation).

| Question | Why it needs the answer |
| :-- | :-- |
| Platform and placements | Decides the ratios, export sizes, lengths, and safe zones |
| The footage | Attach it, or point at it in your creative library |
| The angle | The [angle brief](/angle-brief) carries the proposition and drafted hooks the openings execute |
| Hook per cut | From [hook types](/hook-types), one per variant |
| Length targets | Per placement; the brief or the platform's norms set them |
| Must-include items | Logo treatment, offer line, CTA, any required legal line, from the creative brief's mandatories |

## What you get

An edit brief with a cut list: one row per cut, carrying the placement, ratio, export size, target length, the opening and its hook, the caption and text overlay copy in the brand voice, and the must-include items. It lands in the recorded location for the editor, person or AI tool, to cut against, and the finished cuts run the [creative review checklist](/creative-review-checklist) before anything spends money.

## Runbook

The run card an agent executes this skill from, in order. The turn-by-turn contract is [how the MCP talks](/mcp-conversation).

| Step | What happens |
| :-- | :-- |
| Trigger | Any request to cut, version, or adapt video for ads or placements |
| Ask 1 | Platform and placements. Skip when the campaign or creative brief names them |
| Ask 2 | The footage: attach or point at the library. Skip when attached |
| Ask 3 | The angle, from the [angle library](/angle-library). Skip when the creative brief names it |
| Ask 4 | Hook per cut, offered from [hook types](/hook-types). Skip those the angle brief drafts |
| Ask 5 | Length targets per placement. Skip when the brief sets them |
| Ask 6 | Must-include items. Skip when a filled creative brief is attached; its mandatories list answers this |
| Pulls | The platform's creative specs page and [image and video specs](/image-and-video-specs), the [creative brief](/creative-brief), the [angle brief](/angle-brief), [hook types](/hook-types), [brand voice and tone](/brand-voice-and-tone) for captions and overlays, and the [creative review checklist](/creative-review-checklist) |
| Rules | The three rules above |
| Output | The edit brief and cut list, delivered to the recorded location for the named editor to cut against |

One question per turn, options offered as a pick list, and nothing asked that the documents already answer.
