Run this with the campaign built and paused, before it goes live. LinkedIn's cost per result makes a launch error more expensive here than on the other platforms, so this pass is worth the extra few minutes.

<Info>
  This checklist assumes the [LinkedIn campaign process](/linkedin-campaign-process) has been followed and the [LinkedIn campaign brief](/linkedin-campaign-brief) is complete.
</Info>

## Brief and inputs

- The [LinkedIn campaign brief](/linkedin-campaign-brief) is complete and every linked document resolves.
- The parent [marketing campaign brief](/marketing-campaign-brief) lists LinkedIn in its channels table with a budget line.
- The budget is sized against LinkedIn's cost per result, not copied from another platform's line item.
- Every [customer profile](/customer-profile-library-overview) targeted is linked from the library.
- Copy is approved and sits within the limits in the [copy template](/linkedin-single-image-copy-template) for this format.

## Tracking

- The Insight Tag is installed and **firing**, verified in Campaign Manager.
- The Conversions API is connected, where available.
- The conversion action matches a row in the [event tracking plan](/event-tracking-plan) by name.
- The conversion is attached to this campaign, not just created in the account.
- Every destination URL carries [UTMs](/utm-standards) built to the standard.
- Clicking each ad's URL lands on a live page with no redirect chain.

## Audience

- Estimated audience size is recorded in the brief and is large enough to deliver.
- Audiences across campaigns in this group do not substantially overlap.
- **Audience expansion is set deliberately**, on or off, with the reason recorded.
- **LinkedIn Audience Network is set deliberately**, on or off, with the reason recorded.
- Exclusions are applied: existing customers, current employees, and anything else the brief names.
- For account-based targeting, the company list is uploaded and **at least 300 companies matched**. This should already have been confirmed at brief stage; here it is a re-check against the number in the brief, not the first time anyone looks.
- The list's source, selection criteria, as-of date, refresh date, and retention date are recorded in the [audience list store register](/audience-list-store#the-register).
- No account names, domains, or contact details have been pasted into the brief or any other document.

<Warning>
  Audience expansion and the LinkedIn Audience Network both widen delivery beyond the audience you defined, and both default on in some setups. Left unchecked, they are the most common reason a carefully built account-based campaign delivers to people outside the account list.
</Warning>

## Naming

- Campaign group name matches the [campaign naming conventions](/campaign-naming-conventions).
- Campaign name matches the [campaign naming conventions](/campaign-naming-conventions).
- Audience or variant names match the [ad set / ad group / variant conventions](/ad-set-group-naming-conventions).
- Every ad name matches the [ad naming conventions](/ad-name-conventions).
- If the campaign carries a test, the [experiment ID](/experiment-id-concepts) is appended at the level the hypothesis targets.

## Settings

- Objective matches the brief.
- Budget type and amount match the brief.
- Bid strategy and cost cap value match the brief.
- Flight dates are set and the end date is correct.
- Ad format matches the brief and the copy template used.

## Creative

- Every asset meets the [LinkedIn creative specs](/linkedin-creative-specs) for this format.
- Document ads render correctly page by page in preview.
- Video plays correctly with sound off, with captions where the brief calls for them.
- Every ad has been previewed in Campaign Manager.

## Destination

- If a landing page: it is live, appears in the [landing page library](/landing-page-library), and its conversion action fires on a test submission.
- If a lead form: every field is correct, the privacy policy link resolves, and the thank-you screen and follow-up URL are set.
- **A test lead has been submitted and confirmed arriving in the CRM.**
- The page or form loads correctly on mobile.

## Sign-off

- Approvals recorded, per the [approval process](/approval-process), including any [legal](/legal-review-process) or [brand](/brand-approval-process) review the work needed.
- The owner has confirmed the build against the brief.
- Launch date and mid-flight review date are recorded in the brief.
