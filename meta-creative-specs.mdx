## The 4:5 master

Build the Feed asset at **1080 × 1350** and both other common ratios crop straight out of it. Keep anything that must survive inside the green core.

<Warning>
  Cropping gets you a correctly framed 9:16, not a full-resolution one. For Stories and Reels, shoot or export dedicated vertical creative at 1080 × 1920 rather than upscaling a 759 px crop.
</Warning>

## Placements

| Format | Ratio | Build at | File | Notes |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Feed image** | 1.91:1 to 4:5 | 1080 × 1080 (1:1) or 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | JPG/PNG, max 30 MB | Minimum width 600 px |
| **Feed video** | 4:5 for mobile, 1:1 supported | 1080 × 1350 | MP4/MOV/GIF | Minimum 1080 × 1080. H.264 video, stereo AAC audio |
| **Stories / Reels** | 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | MP4/MOV | Instagram Reels ads can run up to 15 minutes |
| **Carousel** | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 per card | JPG/PNG | Up to 10 cards. Same ratio on every card |
| **In-stream video** | 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 | MP4/MOV | Cut a separate landscape edit |

| Field | Recommended |

| Primary text | 50–150 characters |

## Safe zones

On Stories and Reels, keep logos, pricing, disclaimers, and calls to action clear of the **top and bottom interface areas**. The profile row and username sit at the top, the caption and CTA at the bottom.

<Note>
  Exact pixel extents are not restated here. They vary by placement and by how long your caption runs, and Meta revises them. Check the live safe-zone guides in Ads Manager placement previews before a production run, and preview every placement rather than trusting one.
</Note>

## Working notes

- **Do not auto-crop Feed creative into Stories.** Build dedicated vertical. Automatic crops put the subject behind the interface.
- **Design for sound off.** Captions on Feed video are recommended; do not assume audio plays on start.
- **Carousel cards truncate.** Card text is clipped differently per placement, so preview each one.
- **Do not pillarbox.** For in-stream, cut a real 16:9 edit rather than adding borders to a vertical file.
