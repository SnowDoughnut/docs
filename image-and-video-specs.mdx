## Build one master, export many

Where a platform accepts several ratios of the same creative, compose one master and keep everything important inside the region common to every crop.

| Platform | Master | Core that survives every crop |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Meta | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | 759 × 1080 |
| Google Performance Max | 1200 × 1200 (1:1) | 960 × 628 |

The saving is design time, not upload steps. Platforms still want each ratio as its own file. And a crop is a reframe, not a resolution gain: export full-size vertical for Stories, Reels, and Shorts rather than upscaling a crop.

## A note on safe zones

Meta and TikTok both overlay their interface on full-screen vertical creative, and both move those boundaries over time. TikTok's usable area also changes with caption length and which interactive elements are present, so there is no single correct figure.

These pages show **where** the interface sits and say so plainly; they do not restate pixel counts that would go stale. Pull the current safe-zone template from the platform, since TikTok publishes downloadable ones and Meta shows them in Ads Manager placement previews, and design against it.

Google publishes no percentage safe zones at all. Center the focal subject and assume any edge can crop.
