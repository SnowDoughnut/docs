## Build one master, export many

Most platforms accept several ratios of the same creative. Rather than designing each one, compose a single master and keep everything important inside the region common to every crop.

For Performance Max that master is 1200 × 1200: both other required ratios crop out of it at exactly Google's recommended sizes, leaving a 960 × 628 core that survives everything.

The saving is in design time, not upload steps — platforms still want each ratio as a separate file.

## A note on safe zones

Meta publishes percentage safe zones for Stories and Reels. **Google does not.** Where a platform publishes no safe zone, these pages say so rather than inventing one; centre the focal subject and assume any edge can crop.

## Sources and currency

Ad platforms change specs without notice. Every page names its sources and the date they were checked. Before a large production run, spot-check the linked source pages rather than trusting a cached figure.
