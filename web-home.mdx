Your own site is the one channel where you control the whole page. That removes the platform constraints and puts every decision back on the brief.

## Two kinds of page

| | Where it is registered |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Campaign landing page**, built for one offer, retires with it | [Landing page library](/landing-page-library) |
| **Standing page**, homepage, pricing, product, contact | [Site pages register](/site-pages-register) |

Both carry an owner, a conversion action, a review date, and a record of what points at them. They differ in how they come into existence: a campaign page is briefed and built through the process below, a standing page predates it.

## Pages and articles are different things

A landing page exists to produce one action from traffic you paid to send. An article exists to be found by someone searching. They're briefed differently and measured differently.

If the page has to rank, it's an article: see [write an article](/write-an-article) and [SEO](/seo-home).

## Connect it back

A landing page that isn't linked from its campaign brief becomes an orphan nobody can attribute. Record the live URL on the campaign brief in the [campaigns library](/campaign-library-overview).
