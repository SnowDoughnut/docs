## Pages and articles are different things

A landing page exists to produce one action from traffic you paid to send. An article exists to be found by someone searching. They're briefed differently and measured differently.

If the page has to rank, it's an article: see [write an article](/write-an-article) and [SEO](/seo-home).

## Connect it back

A landing page that isn't linked from its campaign brief becomes an orphan nobody can attribute. Record the live URL on the campaign brief in the [campaigns library](/campaign-library-overview).
