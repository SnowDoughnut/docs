---
title: "Email pre-send checklist"
description: "The last pass before an email goes out: segment and suppressions correct, authentication passing, every link working and tagged, rendering checked in light and dark mode, unsubscribe present."
---

Run this before sending a broadcast or activating a sequence. Email is the one channel here with no pause button: once a send goes out it cannot be recalled, corrected, or paused mid-flight.

<Info>
  This checklist assumes the [email campaign process](/email-campaign-process) has been followed and the [email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) is complete.
</Info>

## Brief and inputs

- The [email campaign brief](/email-campaign-brief) is complete and every linked document resolves.
- The parent [marketing campaign brief](/marketing-campaign-brief) lists email in its channels table.
- Copy is approved and came from the [copy brief](/ad-copy-standards), not written in the platform.
- Design meets the [email creative specs](/email-creative-specs).

## Audience

- The segment matches the definition written in the brief.
- **The contact count matches the estimate in the brief.** A sharp difference means the query doesn't say what you think it says.
- Every suppression in the brief is applied: customers, unsubscribes, hard bounces, and anyone in a competing send this week.
- Every contact has a recorded consent basis.
- The test recipients are excluded from the live send.

<Warning>
  Check the count before sending, every time. A segment that returns ten times the expected contacts is the single most expensive error in email, and it is visible in one number before anything goes out.
</Warning>

## Authentication and deliverability

- SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass on the sending domain.
- The List-Unsubscribe header is present, one-click where supported.
- An unsubscribe link is in the body and it works.
- A physical postal address is in the footer.
- Send volume is within the normal range for this domain, or the domain has been warmed for it.
- An inbox placement or seed test has been run.

## Content

- Subject line matches the brief.
- **Preview text is set**, not falling back to the first line of the email.
- From name, from address, and reply-to match the brief.
- **The reply-to address is monitored by someone.**
- No placeholder text remains anywhere in the email.
- Personalization tokens resolve, and empty-field fallbacks read correctly.
- The offer and call-to-action wording match the landing page exactly.

## Links and tracking

- **Every link has been clicked in a test email**, including links inside images and buttons.
- Every link carries [UTMs](/utm-standards) built to the standard.
- `utm_content` distinguishes this send from the others in its sequence.
- The conversion action matches a row in the [event tracking plan](/event-tracking-plan) by name.
- No link points to a staging or preview URL.

## Rendering

- Tested in at least one webmail client, one mobile app, and one desktop client.
- **Checked in both light and dark mode.** Logos and text remain legible against an inverted background.
- The email is not clipped. Total HTML size is under the threshold in the [email creative specs](/email-creative-specs).
- Images have alt text that reads sensibly when images are blocked.
- The email is readable with images off entirely.

## Naming

- Campaign or flow name matches the [campaign naming conventions](/campaign-naming-conventions).
- Segment name matches the [ad set / ad group / variant conventions](/ad-set-group-naming-conventions).
- Send name matches the [ad naming conventions](/ad-name-conventions).

## Send settings

- Send time and timezone match the brief.
- For a sequence: the trigger, the timing between sends, and the **exit condition** are all set.
- Scheduled date and time are correct, including year.

## Sign-off

- Approvals recorded, per the [approval process](/approval-process), including any [legal](/legal-review-process) or [brand](/brand-approval-process) review the work needed.
- The owner has confirmed the build against the brief.
- For a sequence, a review date is recorded in the brief.
