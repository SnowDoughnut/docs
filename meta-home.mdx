Meta covers Facebook and Instagram placements. Only the platform-specific detail lives here. Strategy, naming, tracking, and brand rules are shared and don't change per platform.

## Launching a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow the process">
    [**Launching a Meta campaign**](/meta-campaign-process) covers campaign, ad set, and ad, and lists every input that has to exist first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the brief">
    The [**Meta campaign brief**](/meta-campaign-brief) fixes objective, conversion action, budget, bid strategy, attribution, and structure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check before you spend">
    The [**Meta pre-launch checklist**](/meta-pre-launch-checklist) is the last pass with the campaign built and paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>
